Privacy Policy · Pogona

Updated: October 18, 2026

When you sign in with Google or an email link, we store your email address and name only to identify your account. We also store what you enter in the app: your reptiles' details, log entries, bulbs, settings and photos you upload.

Data is stored with Supabase (EU servers). Each user can access only their own data, enforced by database row-level security. Photos are kept in private storage. We do not sell or share data with third parties or use it for advertising.

To delete your account and all data, email balulu12@gmail.com.
